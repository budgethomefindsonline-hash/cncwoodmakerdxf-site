Questions

How do I measure my drawer?

Measure the inside width, depth and height at the narrowest point, and leave a small clearance so the organizer drops in easily.

Can I use 4 or 6 mm plywood?

Yes. Enter the real thickness you measure and the slots are calculated from it.

Do I need glue?

The parts slot together. A little wood glue makes the organizer stiffer, especially for heavy cutlery.

Which software opens the files?

The DXF and SVG files work in LightBurn, xTool Creative Space, LaserGRBL, Inkscape and most CAD programs.

Is it free?

Designing and the 3D preview are free. Downloading the cutting files uses a download pack (5, 10 or 20 downloads) bought on my Etsy shop. You enter the code once and the downloads are saved in your browser.
