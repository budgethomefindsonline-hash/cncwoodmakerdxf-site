Questions

What laser do I need?

Any diode or CO₂ laser that cuts 3 mm plywood. If the wall is longer than your bed, split it into 2 or 3 pieces.

What are the layers in the files?

CUT_INNER (cut first), CUT_OUTER, engraving layers and MARK (not cut). Set the power and speed for each layer in LightBurn.

Why cut the test page first?

It checks the pin and slot fit and the bending at the tips and valleys on your plywood before you cut the full box.

Can I sell the boxes I make?

Yes, you can sell the physical boxes. Reselling the files is not allowed.
