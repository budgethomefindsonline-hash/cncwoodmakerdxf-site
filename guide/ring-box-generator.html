Questions

How do I hold the ring inside?

Use a small foam or velvet insert with a slit for the ring. Many makers glue felt to the bottom.

Which lid is best for a proposal?

A hinged lid gives the classic "open the box" moment. A removable lid is the simplest to make.

What material should I use?

3 mm birch plywood looks good. Hardwood veneer plywood or MDF with paint also work.

Which files do I get?

SVG and DXF files in millimetres for LightBurn, xTool, Glowforge and most laser software.
