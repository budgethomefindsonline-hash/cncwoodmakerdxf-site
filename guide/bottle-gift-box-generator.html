Questions

What plywood thickness should I use?

The generator is set up for 3 mm plywood, and you can enter the exact thickness you measure. The joints and the living hinge are calculated from that value.

Will it work with my bottle?

Yes. Enter the height with the cork, the widest diameter, the neck diameter and where the shoulder starts. The preview shows the bottle inside the box, so you can see the fit before cutting.

Which file formats do I get?

DXF, SVG and AI at 1:1 scale in millimetres, ready for LightBurn, xTool Creative Space, Glowforge and most laser software.

Does the flexible top crack?

Living hinges depend on your plywood. Cut the small flex test from the same sheet first, and adjust the slit length or bridge width if it is too stiff.
