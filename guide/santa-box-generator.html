Questions

Is this good for Christmas markets?

Yes. Santa boxes filled with sweets are a popular market item, and you may sell the boxes you make.

What plywood should I use?

3 mm plywood is the default. Enter the thickness you measure, because real plywood is often 2.8–3.2 mm.

Do the walls bend without cracking?

The walls use a living-hinge pattern. Bending depends on your plywood, so cut the curve test first.

Which files do I get?

SVG and DXF files in millimetres, with separate layers for engraving, inner cuts and outer cuts.
