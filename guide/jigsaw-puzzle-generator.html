Questions

What material works best?

3 mm plywood or MDF works well. Thinner sheets are easier for a diode laser to cut cleanly.

Can I make a puzzle for a small child?

Yes, choose fewer, larger pieces. Sand the edges and use a child-safe finish.

Should I engrave or cut first?

Engrave first while the sheet is still in one piece, then cut. That keeps the picture aligned.

Which formats are included?

SVG and DXF files at 1:1 scale for most laser software.
