Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This website is run by Vadim, trading as CNCWoodMakerDXF, in London, United Kingdom. This notice explains what I collect through the site and what I do with it.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, I keep your email address and use it only to tell you about new generators and designs. I don't sell or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any update or by messaging me, and I will delete your address.

Build photos

If you send a build photo, I receive your name, email, photo and message. With your permission, I may show the photo and your name on this site and on my TikTok and Instagram. I never publish your email. Ask me at any time and I will remove your photo and delete your submission.

Where the data is kept

Form submissions are stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this site, which may process data in the United States. Visitor statistics are collected by Netlify on its servers, without cookies. The page fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

Generators and download packs

The generators run entirely in your browser. The designs you make and your remaining download credits are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to me.

Purchases

Payments happen on Etsy, under Etsy's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent. To do this, message me through my Etsy shop. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
