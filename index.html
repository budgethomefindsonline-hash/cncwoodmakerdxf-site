FAQ

Good to know

How do I get the files after buying?

Etsy gives you an instant download link right after payment, and the files stay in your Etsy purchases.

Which lasers and software are supported?

Any laser that reads DXF or SVG, whether diode, CO₂ or fibre. The files are prepared in LightBurn and also open in xTool Creative Space, Glowforge, Inkscape and LaserGRBL.

Do the files include laser settings?

No. Power and speed depend on your machine and material, so always run a small test cut first.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes, you can sell the physical items you cut from my files. Reselling or sharing the digital files themselves is not allowed.

Are the generators really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up and no download limit.
